Eligibility requirements

Check before making payment

Age at actual graduation

Your age must be 30 years or younger on the actual date of graduation.

Institution restriction

Applicants from National Open University of Nigeria (NOUN) cannot proceed.

Exact dates required

Current age and graduation year alone are not used for eligibility.

Example

An applicant who graduated at age 27 in 2015 remains eligible even if they are currently older.

Eligibility is calculated from date of birth and the actual graduation date.
